Pete Hinchley: Colophon

This is an old school website. The kind you may recall from the good old days. Just a collection of interlinked HTML pages. No JavaScript. No fancy web frameworks. Heck, you don't even need a web server. Clone the site to your computer, double click index.html, and you are up and running. How rare is that!

Each page is composed of dilligently formatted semantic markup. The list-based pages (movies, books, log, etc) are even available in multiple formats. Replace the .html extension with .txt to get a text-based version. Use .json for JSON, and .xml for an Atom feed if you want to subscribe to updates via a feed reader.

By keeping pages lightweight, it is possible to avoid futher bloat, like custom search, pagination, and infinite scroll. The entire tweet archive of over 2,000 entries is a single page, and it comes in at less than 500KB, which is about 1/6th the size of the typical web site home page. Need to find something - just hit ctrl-f to use the native search functionality of the browser.

The site adapts to both desktop and mobile form-factors. It also auto-switches between light and dark mode based on the settings of your operating system.

No data is collected via this site other than the typical activity data logged by the web server. The content and opinions expressed here are my own, and not those of a babbling bot, or some other random person on the interwebs. There are no comment forms, ping backs, or other forms of content federation (beyond Atom feeds). If you want to chat about anything I have written, send me an email, and we can discuss out of band. Corrections and amendments will be made with attribution where warranted.

The content on this site, except that attributed to others (poems, stories, etc), is covered by the Creative Common CC0 waiver. Despite the open stance, robots.txt is used to block some of the more pernicious data scrapers and other autonomous agents hell bent on slurping up the internet.
